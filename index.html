About Me

Passionate about sound and dedicated to the craft, I bring a unique blend of instrumental proficiency and production expertise to every project.

My Journey in Music

From a young age, I've been captivated by the power of music. My journey began with learning various instruments, which shaped my understanding of melody, harmony, and rhythm. This foundation naturally led me into the world of music production, where I discovered the thrill of transforming raw ideas into polished tracks.

Based in Thrissur, Kerala, I continuously explore new sounds and techniques, committed to delivering high-quality, impactful music. Whether it's composing, arranging, recording, mixing, or mastering, my goal is to help artists achieve their sonic vision.

My Creative Workflow

My process is designed to be collaborative, efficient, and focused on delivering exceptional results that resonate with your artistic vision.

1. Discovery & Concept

We start with a detailed discussion to understand your vision, goals, and musical preferences. This phase is crucial for aligning our creative directions.

2. Creation & Iteration

Based on our concept, I begin the creative process, composing, arranging, and producing. We'll have regular check-ins for feedback and refinements.

3. Refine & Deliver

The final stages involve polishing the tracks, performing mixing and mastering, and ensuring the final product exceeds your expectations before delivery.
